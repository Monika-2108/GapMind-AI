
# 🔴 GAPMIND AI
### Don't Just Discover Information. Discover What's Missing.

**AI-Assisted Information Gap Detection & Evidence Intelligence Platform**

> The biggest challenge in research is not always finding information. It is recognising what important information is still missing.

---

## 🚨 THE PROBLEM

We live in a world overflowing with information. Reports, articles, research papers and online publications provide thousands of facts every day.

But more information does not necessarily mean a complete understanding.

Researchers often struggle to identify:

- Important information that is missing.
- Claims that lack sufficient supporting evidence.
- Potentially conflicting information across sources.
- Outdated details that need fresh verification.
- Questions that remain unanswered after research.

Most research tools focus on finding and summarising information. The deeper challenge is understanding what the available information does not tell us.

## 💡 OUR SOLUTION — GAPMIND AI

GapMind AI is an AI-powered Information Gap Detection Platform that transforms multi-source research into structured evidence intelligence.

Rather than simply summarising what sources say, GapMind AI examines individual claims, connects them with source references, identifies potential research gaps and helps users decide what needs further investigation.

**Our central idea: Turn information gaps into actionable research tasks.**

GapMind AI asks two important questions:

1. What information have we found?
2. What important information are we still missing?

---

## ✨ WHAT MAKES GAPMIND AI DIFFERENT?

Traditional research tools help users collect and understand available information.

GapMind AI adds another layer: identifying the blind spots within that information.

| Traditional research | GapMind AI |
|---|---|
| Collects information | Examines evidence across sources |
| Summarises documents | Connects individual claims to sources |
| Presents available findings | Highlights unanswered questions |
| Requires manual comparison | Identifies potential contradictions |
| Leaves further research to users | Creates prioritised verification tasks |

### Our Innovation: The Research Blind-Spot Approach

GapMind AI focuses on the space between what is known, what is supported and what remains unanswered.

Its approach combines:

1. **Claim-Level Evidence Intelligence:** Connects extracted claims with source references and available supporting excerpts.
2. **Information Gap Detection:** Identifies missing details and unanswered research questions.
3. **Potential Contradiction Analysis:** Highlights claims that may conflict and require human review.
4. **Freshness Awareness:** Identifies information that may require updated sources.
5. **Actionable Verification:** Converts research gaps into prioritised verification tasks.

Our goal is not to make AI decide what is true. It is to help people see what still needs to be checked.

## 🔄 HOW IT WORKS

**STEP 1 — Define the Research Topic**

The user enters a research topic or question.

**STEP 2 — Collect Sources**

GapMind AI discovers relevant sources and accepts user-provided research materials.

**STEP 3 — Extract Readable Content**

The platform extracts available content from webpages and documents. Limited search previews are kept separate from complete readable evidence.

**STEP 4 — Analyse Claims**

Google Gemini analyses collected content and organises claims, findings and unanswered questions.

**STEP 5 — Map the Evidence**

Claims are connected to source references and available supporting excerpts.

**STEP 6 — Detect Research Blind Spots**

The platform identifies information gaps, potential contradictions and freshness concerns.

**STEP 7 — Take Action**

A prioritised verification checklist guides users towards the next research steps.

## 🖥️ INSTALLATION & SETUP

### Prerequisites
- Python 3.10 or later
- Google Gemini API key

### 1. Clone the repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Monika-2108/GapMind-AI.git
```

### 2. Enter the project directory

```bash
cd GapMind-AI
```

### 3. Create a virtual environment

```bash
python -m venv .venv
```

### 4. Activate the environment (Windows PowerShell)

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
```

### 5. Install dependencies

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
```

### 6. Configure the Gemini API key

Create a `.env` file in the project root using `.env.example` as a template.

Add your own API key:

```env
GEMINI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key_here
```

Never publish your actual API key.

### 7. Run the application

```bash
streamlit run app.py
```

## 🔐 RESPONSIBLE AI & LIMITATIONS

GapMind AI is a research-support platform, not an independent fact-checking authority.

- AI-generated findings require human review.
- A source reference does not automatically prove a claim.
- Potential contradictions are signals for investigation, not confirmed falsehoods.
- Evidence coverage measures source-link relationships, not factual accuracy.
- Results depend on the quality and accessibility of collected sources.
- Limited search previews are not treated as complete source documents.

We believe useful AI should make uncertainty visible rather than hide it.
